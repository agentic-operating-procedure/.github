# Security Policy

## Scope

This policy covers every repository in the `agentic-operating-procedure` organisation.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately through a security advisory on the affected repository. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

Include:

- The repository and, if applicable, the specific artefact.
- A description of the issue and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- Any suggested remediation.

You will receive an acknowledgement within three working days and a status update at least every seven days until the issue is resolved.

## What to report

Report anything that would allow an actor to exceed the authority an Agentic Operating Procedure grants it, to bypass an authority check, to tamper with an evidence record, to forge provenance, or to cause a published artefact to behave differently from its specification.

The programme treats an authority-boundary bypass as a defect in the governing artefact, not only in the code, and the fix is expected to land in the artefact that owns the rule.

## Out of scope

- Findings that depend on a model or system being unavailable.
- Vulnerabilities in third-party dependencies with no programme-specific amplification.
- Reports without a demonstrated impact on the authority model or the assurance obligations.

## Disclosure

Coordinated disclosure is requested. Please allow time for a fix and a release before public disclosure.
